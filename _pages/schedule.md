## Things to Enjoy While You’re in San Diego

__Mission Bay and Pacific Beach offer countless ways to enjoy the long weekend, including:__

• Paddleboarding or kayaking on the calm waters of Mission Bay

• Jet skis, electric boats, or biking the scenic bay path

• Relaxing on the beaches of Pacific Beach



__You might also enjoy exploring some of San Diego’s highlights:__

• San Diego Zoo – one of the best zoos in the world

• La Jolla Cove – beautiful coastal views and sea lions

• Balboa Park – gardens, museums, and historic architecture

• Old Town San Diego – great Mexican food, tequila tastings, music, and historic adobe buildings



__Labor Day Weekend Special Event__

At the Broadway Pier Sand Sculpting Festival, artists from around the world create enormous sculptures using over 300 tons of sand. It’s considered one of the top seaside sand-sculpture events in the world and is a fun Labor Day tradition in San Diego.
